Reflection · Mar 2024

Death of a Salesman

by Arthur Miller
Completed: Mar 2024
Rating: 9.6/10
Type: Personal Reflection
01 / Verdict

Tethering one’s worth to external influences deprives life of its inherent meaning, emphasizing the imperative of basing personal fulfillment on individual values rather than the societal rat race.

— me, Mar 2024
02 / Story

Arthur Miller's Pulitzer Prize-winning tragedy follows Willy Loman, an aging Brooklyn salesman whose desperate obsession with being well-liked and achieving material wealth leads to catastrophic self-delusion, family dysfunction, and the tragic unraveling of his life.

03 / Resonance

Writing during the post-WWII surge of consumerism and material optimism, Arthur Miller witnessed the pernicious consequences of the notion that anyone could achieve even the loftiest financial goals, often resulting in despair when failing to meet utopian expectations. Through Biff's realization that his life built on external validation was a "ridiculous lie," Miller emphasizes the imperative of basing personal fulfillment on individual values rather than expectations mandated by society. Biff's refusal to conform to a stable office job highlights the necessity of seeking authenticity over commercial conformity.

04 / Reflection

Miller contrasts Willy's delusion with his brother Ben, who views money as a conduit for personal fulfillment rather than an end in itself. Ben’s aphorism that the jungle is "dark but full of diamonds" underscores that true accomplishment requires unconventional risk and personal contentment, rather than blindly conforming to corporate hierarchies. In contrast, Willy's commodification of his own life - ultimately viewing his suicide as a final financial transaction to cash in on life insurance - marks the ultimate indictment of consumerist culture.

05 / Takeaway

Miller spotlights the deleterious effects of blindly following the ideals of the American Dream through the downfall, failures, and triumphs of his characters. In contemporary society, the prominence of e-commerce platforms streamlines spending and exacerbates consumerism. Miller’s message accentuates the imperative of preserving individuality and autonomy, resisting conformity to the societal rat race, and defining success by personal contentment rather than external social standards.
